Draft, effective when memberships open. A California lawyer must review it before it is used at sign-up. Placeholders in [brackets] must be filled in with the real legal name, mailing address, and contact email.

Operator: [Legal name], doing business as SeatKind ("SeatKind," "we," "us") Contact: [privacy@ or hello@ email] · [mailing address, California] Service: the SeatKind website and app for adults 21 and older in the Modesto and Turlock, California area.

1. What this policy covers

This policy describes how we would collect, use, and share personal information if SeatKind were live. This draft site and these forms are not connected and do not collect or store your information.

2. Information we would collect

  • Account and contact: name, email, phone number (for a one-time verification code).
  • City or area only (for example, "Modesto" or "Turlock area"). We do not ask for a street address or precise GPS on your profile, and we do not show a precise location to other members.
  • 21+ review information: an attested date of birth; a profile photo; a selfie in which you hold a handwritten note with the day's date and your first name; and a record that a person reviewed your file. We use date of birth to confirm you attested to being 21 or older. We show your age to other members only as a decade bucket (20s, 30s, 40s, 50s, or 60s), never as an exact age. For this product, "20s" means ages 21–29.
  • Preferences: interests, the kind of friend you want, whom you are open to meeting (men, women, or anyone, for friendship), availability, and age-range preferences in decade buckets (21+ minimum).
  • Payment: handled by Stripe. We would receive a Stripe customer and subscription id, payment status (active, past_due, canceled, and similar), and limited billing metadata. We do not want to store full card numbers. Stripe's privacy policy would also apply.
  • Use of the service: messages you send in the app, reports and blocks, table invites, device or log data needed to operate and secure the service.
  • If you contact us: the content of that message.

We do not run government-ID or license checks or background checks. We do not build a face template or use your selfie for biometric identification or facial age estimation. The selfie is only for a person to compare to your profile photos and then be deleted as described below.

3. How we would use it

  • To create and review your profile, including the 21+ review stack: attested date of birth, phone verification, paid membership, selfie-with-date, and human review of inconsistencies.
  • To operate matching, messaging, table invites, and membership billing (including auto-renewal, failed-payment retries, and cancellation).
  • To review reports and enforce the community rules.
  • To send service messages (review results, receipts, renewal reminders, cancel confirmations). We would not use your information to pitch dating or unrelated products.
  • To secure the service and comply with law.

4. Selfies and deletion after review

After a person finishes the 21+ review (approved or declined), we would delete the selfie image. We may keep a record that a selfie was submitted, the date of review, the decision, and a short note about inconsistencies, without keeping the image. Profile photos you choose to show on your profile are kept while the account is open, and for a short period after closure as needed for safety or legal reasons. Verify retention periods with counsel.

5. We do not sell your data

We would not sell your personal information, and we would not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We would not use it to train a public AI model.

We may use processors who help us run the service (for example Stripe for payments, an SMS provider for one-time codes, email delivery, and hosting). They would be allowed to use your information only to provide that service to us.

6. California privacy rights

Even if SeatKind is not yet a "business" under the CCPA/CPRA (the current gross-revenue threshold is $26,625,000, or 100,000 consumers whose information is bought, sold, or shared, or 50% of revenue from selling or sharing personal information — verify current figures at the CPPA threshold page), we would still aim to honor, on request: access, correction, deletion, and a limit on any unexpected use of sensitive information. To make a request, write to [privacy email]. We would not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

7. Children

SeatKind is only for adults 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 21, and we do not direct the service at children under 13 (COPPA). If we learn someone under 21 has an account, we would close it and delete the associated personal information, except where we must keep a limited record.

8. How long we would keep information

We would keep account, preference, and message data while your account is open, and for a limited time after that if needed for safety, billing disputes, or legal obligations (including keeping a record of automatic-renewal consent as required by California law — verify the period with counsel). Selfies are deleted after review, as in section 4.

9. Security and location

We would use reasonable technical and organizational measures. No method is perfectly secure. Profiles show a city or area only, never a precise location.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy is adopted, we would post updates on this page and, if the change is material, notify you by email. Questions: [privacy email] or [mailing address].

This document is a draft for discussion. It is not legal advice and is not a live privacy policy.
