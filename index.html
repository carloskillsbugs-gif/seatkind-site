How it works

Match instantly, chat in the app, meet at a table

Tell us the kind of friend you want. We find you someone instantly — a match happens only when that interest is mutual. Then you talk in the app before you sit down together.

  1. 1

    Match instantly

    You pick interests, vibe, availability, whom you're open to meeting, and age-range decades (20s through 60s, 21 and up). A match happens when it is mutual — you both tap “Want to be friends?” — and the match screen shows why you fit. Near matches show a match percentage and shared interests; they do not have to be perfect.

  2. 2

    Chat in the app

    Matched people can talk back and forth in SeatKind messaging before they meet. Keep the conversation in the app. You can report or block anyone, and we review every report within 24 hours.

  3. 3

    Meet at a table

    When you are ready, send a table invite and meet at a reserved public restaurant in Modesto or Turlock. If it feels right, you can come back. Tables are included in membership or a small add-on — that part is still TBD.

Safety

A kind community, with clear rules

Meeting new people should feel steady and respectful, in the app and at the table. We do not run ID or license checks, and we do not run background checks. We say that plainly.

21+ review, not an ID check

Every profile is reviewed by a person before it goes live. The stack is: an attested date of birth, phone verification, a paid membership, a selfie holding today's date, and a human look at anything that does not add up. Until that review is done, your profile stays under review and cannot match or chat.

SeatKind Approved is a pledge

SeatKind Approved is a marketing label, not a trademark and not a safety certification. It means the person agreed to follow the law and our community rules, and their profile passed the 21+ review above. It is not identity verification, not a background check, and it does not mean someone is safe to meet.

Report and block, reviewed in 24 hours

You can report or block someone in the app. We review every report within 24 hours. If it is an emergency, contact local emergency services first. Keep chats in SeatKind until you meet at a public table.

City or area only

Profiles show a city or area, such as Modesto or Turlock — never a precise location, home address, or “nearby” pin. Tables happen at a reserved seat in a public restaurant, never at a private home. A SeatKind host is there with you.

You can leave, and you can tell us

You can step away at any time. If something feels wrong in a chat or at a table, report it in the app, tell the host, or write to us.

An honest limit: this review stack can catch inconsistencies and keep a 21+ standard in view. It cannot prove legal age or identity, and some people will still lie. We will not advertise SeatKind Approved as “verified ID,” a background check, or “safe to meet.”

The fine print, in plain words

Privacy, rules, and membership terms

Privacy

This public site does not collect submissions. When memberships open, SeatKind would collect:

  • Your name, email, and phone, so we can verify the phone, reach you, and operate the membership
  • Your city or area only (for example Modesto or Turlock) — not a precise location
  • Your attested date of birth (your profile will show a decade bucket such as 20s or 30s, not an exact age), a profile photo, and a selfie with today's date, for the 21+ review. The selfie would be deleted after review.
  • The friend preferences you choose, so matches can be mutual
  • In-app messages, as needed to operate chat and to review a report
  • Payment details through a payment provider, if you start a membership

We would not sell this information, and we would not use it to pitch dating or unrelated products. You could ask to be removed at any time by writing to us when a support email is posted here. Memberships aren't open yet, and this site does not collect messages.

Code of conduct

Everyone who uses SeatKind, in the app and at a table, agrees to the following:

  • Come to make friends, not dates. No romantic pitches, pickup lines, or pressure to exchange numbers for dating.
  • Keep chats in the SeatKind app until you meet at the public table. Do not pressure anyone to move the conversation off the app.
  • Be kind. Listen as much as you talk, and leave room for quieter people.
  • Respect boundaries, names, and pronouns. No harassment, hate, or unwanted contact. You can report or block someone in the app; we review reports within 24 hours.
  • Alcohol, if it is served, is a personal choice. Never pressure anyone to drink.
  • Meet only at the reserved public restaurant table — not at someone's home, car, or a last-minute private place.
  • You may leave at any time. The host may end a night early or ask someone to leave if the code is broken.

This code will be refined before launch. Breaking it can mean losing a seat at future tables or a banned profile.

A few straightforward questions

Is SeatKind Approved a background check or ID check?

No. SeatKind Approved is a community pledge plus a 21+ profile review. It is not identity verification, not a background check, and it does not mean a person is safe to meet. We do not ask for a government ID or license, and we do not run background checks.

How does the 21+ review work?

You attest your date of birth, verify your phone, start a paid membership, and send a selfie holding today's date. A person then reviews the file for inconsistencies. Any doubt about age means the profile does not go live until it is resolved. This is a serious stack of checks, not a proof of legal age.

How do I cancel, and what is the founding price?

Standard membership is $20 a month. The first 100 members pay $15 a month, locked only while they stay continuously subscribed; if they cancel and return later, they pay $20. Cancel online anytime, with no call required — when billing is live, through a Stripe Customer Portal link and in the app. A preview is on the cancel page. Are the profiles on this page real members?

What can I do if something goes wrong?
