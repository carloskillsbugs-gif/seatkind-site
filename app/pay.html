In production this box is replaced by Stripe. SeatKind would not store full card numbers. Failed renewals would be retried by Stripe (dunning / Smart Retries), and you could update the card in the Customer Portal.

Automatic renewal — please read this, next to the pay button.

SeatKind is a monthly membership. If you continue, you will be charged $15.00 today, and then $15.00 every month until you cancel online. You can cancel anytime, with no phone call, through the billing portal (and in the app). If you cancel, you keep access through the period you already paid for; we do not generally prorate a mid-cycle cancel. After you join, we will email you a copy of these terms and how to cancel.

If you are a founding member and you let the subscription end, a later membership is billed at the standard $20.00 per month.
